# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a security vulnerability. Email
security@nemau-cloud.com instead.

Include the provider version, the Terraform version, what you ran, what you
observed and, if you can, the steps to reproduce it. Reports in French or
English are accepted.

The disclosure policy is published at
https://nemau-cloud.com/en/security#responsible-disclosure.
